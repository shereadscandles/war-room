Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026 · Back to War Room

What we store

  • Your login: email address and a securely hashed password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Your app data: settings, journal tags and trade plans, so they follow you across devices. Only your own login can read them.
  • Your Alpaca connection: the access token Alpaca issues when you approve War Room, stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on the server and never sent to your browser.

What we never store

Your Alpaca password, your Alpaca API keys, or any AI keys you enter. Keys you type into Settings stay on that device only.

How it’s used

Your data is used only to run War Room for you: showing your account, placing orders you confirm, and building your journal from your Alpaca fills. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Supabase (database and login hosting), Alpaca (your brokerage, via its API), and GitHub Pages (hosts the app’s files). If you use “Ask,” your question and the trading context you choose to send go to Anthropic using your own API key.

Your choices

Disconnect Alpaca anytime in Settings or in your Alpaca dashboard. To delete your account and data, contact the operator and it will be removed.

Security

Server-only tables are locked so browsers can’t read them, connections use HTTPS, and the server only relays a fixed list of trading and market-data requests.
